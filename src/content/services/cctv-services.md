We survey the site before quoting, because the number of cameras matters far less than where they point, how long the footage is kept, and whether anyone can actually retrieve it when it is needed.

We supply and install Hikvision, Dahua, Uniview and EZVIZ systems, configure recording and retention to suit the site, and set up secure remote viewing. We also take on existing systems that were installed badly by someone else — fault-finding, re-cabling and reconfiguration.

Access control and biometric entry systems are handled by the same team.
