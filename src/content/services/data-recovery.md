If a drive is making noise, has stopped mounting, or you have deleted something that mattered, the single most useful thing you can do is stop using it and bring it in.

Continued use overwrites exactly the data you are trying to get back. A drive that is mechanically failing also degrades further every time it is spun up.

We assess first and tell you honestly what is recoverable before any work is agreed. Recovery from a dead laptop where the drive itself is healthy is usually straightforward and quick.
