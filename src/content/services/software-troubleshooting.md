A machine that is slow, crashing or refusing to boot is not necessarily a broken machine. Failing drivers, corrupt system files, malware, full disks and bad updates produce symptoms that look exactly like hardware failure.

We separate the two before spending your money. Where the fix is software, we repair or reinstall and migrate your data across. Where it turns out to be hardware after all, the diagnosis has already been done.

We also supply and license genuine antivirus, security and productivity software.
