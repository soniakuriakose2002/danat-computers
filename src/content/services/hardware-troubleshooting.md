Intermittent faults are the hardest kind and the most commonly misdiagnosed. A machine that restarts under load, throttles after ten minutes or fails to charge on one cable but not another needs testing, not guesswork.

We test components in isolation — power rails, memory, storage, thermals, display chains — and tell you which one is actually failing.

Upgrades are the same conversation in reverse: often a machine written off as too slow needs an SSD and more memory, not replacing.
