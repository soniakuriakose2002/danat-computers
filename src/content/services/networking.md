We design, install and repair wired and wireless networks for offices, shops, clinics and warehouses.

That covers the unglamorous part — structured cabling, patch panels, cabinets, terminations — as well as the equipment: managed switches, PoE for cameras and phones, access points placed by survey rather than by guesswork, routers and firewalls.

If you already have a network that drops, slows or will not reach the far end of the building, we will trace it and tell you what is actually wrong.
