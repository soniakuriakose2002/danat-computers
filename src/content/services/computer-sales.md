We sell new machines from the brands you would expect — HP, Dell, Acer, ASUS, Lenovo, Apple — and we sell refurbished ones alongside them.

Refurbished is not a lesser shelf. Every unit is tested and warranted, and for most offices it is the difference between replacing four machines and replacing one.

Behind the retail counter is a wholesale operation: hospitals, businesses and other computer retailers across Oman buy supply, parts and consumables from us at trade. If you need a quantity rather than a unit, talk to us directly.
